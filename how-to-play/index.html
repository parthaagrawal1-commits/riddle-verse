How to play

Three puzzles, one world Every day at 00:00 UTC, three new puzzles go live for everyone on Earth at the same moment. Same puzzles, same clock, one global leaderboard.

  • Riddle. A classic or original riddle. Type your answer; spelling is forgiving and we accept sensible variants. Three hints are available; each one you use costs a star.
  • Rebus. A picture puzzle in emoji. Say the pictures out loud and the phrase appears.
  • Chain. Five short clues. The first letters of your five answers spell a final word.

Scoring Three stars for a solve with no hints, two with one hint, one with two. The leaderboard ranks by fewest hints, then fastest time. Your streak counts consecutive days with at least one solve.

Sharing After each puzzle, **Share** copies a spoiler-free result card. **Challenge a friend** sends a link with your time, so they play the same puzzle against your clock.

Fair play Answers are stored as one-way hashes, so the page source can't spoil you. Names on the leaderboard are optional and anything goes within 16 characters; be nice.
